## Unresolved decisions

The exact Earn narrow composition will follow direct inspection. An information
cell can be shared without sharing the underlying vote-lock and staking data or
transaction mechanics. APR and APY cannot be silently unified; missing wallet
data cannot be represented as None or zero. Source legacy fallback behavior is
an adoption issue, not permission to change product correctness overnight.

The strongest case against immediate Earn expansion is that current specimens
may be incomplete and another attractive mock would multiply that failure.
The source-first gate and retained repro evidence address this; if the source
cannot be rendered faithfully enough, finish its evidence/preparation instead
of filling missing context with invented interactions.

## Experience and transfer contract

Usage: browse governed assets and the rate's period/meaning while disconnected;
connect a synthetic portfolio to compare the same opportunities with existing
holdings; inspect multiple governed DTFs or rate help without activating the row.
Recover from wallet loading/unavailable without changing known TVL or identity.

One local candidate is sufficient. Reuse DataTable's existing sorting owner and
accepted component APIs; reject a new global table abstraction, two independent
responsive sort owners, and importing data hooks into cells. This is a reversible
composition decision, not an Arena comparison or a shared ownership redesign.

Visible evaluation rubric: source fidelity; value/period honesty; balanced full
composition; keyboard and nested-action isolation; simple local ownership.
Long governed names, more than three governed assets, a missing wallet price and
resize while a disclosure is focused are visible pressure cases, not held-out
independent evidence. The final presentation remains human-review-required.

The detailed production-transfer matrix, exact component and geometry ownership,
state continuity, truth categories and first rendered slice will be recorded in
the Earn slice brief after source observation, before candidate implementation.
